02 – PARTNERS

Gökhan Er

Co-Founder & Managing Partner

Investing in crypto since 2017 with a focus on early-stage infrastructure. Gökhan spent four years as Managing Director at IOSG Ventures, and before that worked on strategy at Huobi Global's Beijing headquarters as one of the firm's first foreign employees. He earned his Master's from Peking University with a thesis on China's role in global crypto markets. Fluent in Mandarin, English, and Turkish, he spends most of his time in Hong Kong after 8+ years in Beijing and Shenzhen.

Tomasz K. Stańczak

Co-Founder & General Partner

Building at the core of Ethereum since 2017, Tomasz is a Co-Founder and General Partner of Lantern Capital through Nethermind, and currently serves as Co-Executive Director at the Ethereum Foundation. He founded Nethermind, a leading crypto research and engineering firm, and served as CEO of Twinstake, an institutional staking platform with over $2B in AUM. He has held governance roles at the Starknet Foundation and Forta, and was an early team member at Flashbots.
